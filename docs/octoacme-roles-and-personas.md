# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test automation direction, and acceptance validation. They partner with delivery roles to ensure features meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and evolve the project test strategy
- Maintain and improve automated test suites
- Validate implemented features against acceptance criteria
- Identify quality risks and recommend mitigations
- Conduct or coordinate manual QA as needed

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Increase confidence in release readiness
- Improve test effectiveness and execution reliability

### Typical Communication
- Test strategy and quality risk reviews with Developers
- Acceptance criteria validation feedback with Product Managers
- Quality status, defect trends, and test-readiness updates with Project Managers

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads/Architects provide architectural guidance, code review oversight, and technical risk assessment across project initiatives. They help the team make sound technical decisions aligned with delivery goals.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and implementation approaches
- Identify technical risks and cross-system dependencies
- Guide architecture choices and engineering standards
- Mentor developers through design and code review practices
- Support estimation during planning with technical input

### Goals
- Maintain a scalable, maintainable architecture
- Reduce technical uncertainty and rework
- Improve delivery predictability through better technical planning

### Typical Communication
- Design and code review discussions with Developers
- Feasibility and trade-off discussions with Product Managers
- Technical risk and dependency updates with Project Managers

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities, verify release readiness, and orchestrate production deployments. They ensure releases are executed safely and consistently.

### Responsibilities
- Verify pre-release requirements and sign-off checkpoints
- Coordinate deployment windows and release sequencing
- Execute or coordinate production deployments
- Run post-deployment verification and incident checks
- Manage rollback procedures when needed
- Document and publish release notes

### Goals
- Deliver reliable and low-risk production releases
- Minimize deployment disruption and recovery time
- Improve release traceability and operational confidence

### Typical Communication
- Deployment readiness and handoff coordination with Developers
- Scope confirmation and release acceptance alignment with Product Managers
- Release plans, go/no-go status, and post-release reports with Project Managers

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders/Sponsors provide strategic alignment, business context, and decision authority for project outcomes. They ensure delivery priorities remain aligned with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and expected outcomes
- Approve significant scope or priority changes
- Remove business-level blockers and escalations
- Provide prioritization feedback for roadmap decisions
- Review project status and major risks
- Communicate outcomes to broader organizational groups

### Goals
- Ensure project outcomes deliver measurable business value
- Keep investments aligned with strategic priorities
- Enable timely decisions on scope, risk, and trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Business context and outcome feedback sessions with Developers
- Priority and value alignment discussions with Product Managers
- Status, risk, and decision-point reviews with Project Managers
