## Purpose
Provide a single entry point to the OctoAcme project management process documentation.

## Project Management Process Summary
OctoAcme uses a lightweight but structured project management approach that emphasizes clear ownership, iterative delivery, risk management, regular communication, and continuous improvement. Work typically begins with initiation, where the team confirms the business need, identifies stakeholders, defines success metrics, and decides whether to proceed. Planning then turns the approved initiative into an actionable backlog by breaking work into shippable increments, estimating effort, defining acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done, and mapping dependencies and milestones.

During execution, the team tracks work on a project board, keeps PRs small and reviewable, and uses CI, linting, tests, and manual QA as needed to protect quality. Communication stays frequent and structured through daily standups, weekly syncs, stakeholder updates, demos, and explicit blocker escalation paths. Releases require passing checks, release notes, rollback planning, and post-deploy verification, while retrospectives convert learnings into backlog items and continuous improvements.
